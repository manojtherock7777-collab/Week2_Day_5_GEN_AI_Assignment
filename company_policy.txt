Acme Tech India - Employee Handbook (2026)

1. Work Hours & Hybrid Policy
Core working hours are 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST, Monday through Friday. Employees in the Chennai and Bengaluru offices work hybrid: Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday are in-office days, while Monday and Friday are remote.

2. Leave & Holidays
Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave and 12 public holidays per calendar year. Unused annual leave up to 10 days can be carried forward to the next year.

3. IT & Expense Reimbursement
New joiners receive a laptop allowance of INR 85,000. Home internet bills are reimbursed up to INR 1,500 per month upon submitting receipts in the HR portal before the 25th of each month.
